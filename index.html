Five days of light

  1. Dhanteras. Buy something new and welcome good fortune.
  2. Choti Diwali. Early-morning baths and the first diyas of the season.
  3. Lakshmi Puja. The main night: prayers, sweets and a glowing home.
  4. Govardhan Puja. A day of gratitude and a table full of food.
  5. Bhai Dooj. Siblings celebrate the bond between them.
